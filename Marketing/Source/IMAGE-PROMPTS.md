## Golden carp icon

Original generated file: `exec-17186d6a-6ce7-4a26-ac83-07489896b624.png`.

Workspace source: `../Remotion/public/identity/golden-carp-master.png`.

Exact prompt:

> Create ONE finished premium mobile game app icon for RIVERLIGHT, a cozy low-poly 3D fishing adventure. Square 1:1, 1024x1024 master, edge-to-edge opaque artwork, NO text, NO letters, NO human, NO face, NO person, NO rounded-corner mask, NO external drop shadow. Hero subject: one beautiful golden carp curving upward in a clean dynamic arc over deep jade/teal river water, elegant gold fins, a few luminous amber droplets and a broad sculptural ripple. Fish must look like a warmly lit polished stylized 3D game collectible, tactile hand-carved facets, refined not cartoony baby mascot, no angry expression. Focus on an instantly readable bold silhouette and a striking warm-gold versus rich-teal contrast at 48 pixels. Composition: fish fills central 72%, clearly defined gold scales in broad simple shapes, tail arcing lower-left, head facing upper-right, one small lily leaf in background if useful, soft cinematic late-afternoon rim light, dark teal corners. Art direction: Nintendo-quality cozy indie adventure key art, elevated natural materials, minimal visual noise, rich physically plausible rendered reflections. Subject safely inside center 80% so Play Store corner clipping is harmless. No trophies, badges, UI, rankings, store logos, gradients used as a substitute for art, or fishhook through fish.

> Create ONE ultra-wide premium illustrated key art scene for the mobile game RIVERLIGHT, a cozy low-poly 3D fishing adventure about inheriting Grandpa's riverside cabin, rowboat and old fishing journal. Render as an elevated hand-crafted 3D indie game diorama: clean geometric shapes, beveled wood, tactile materials, polished stylized lighting, refined miniature landscape, richly artistic but plausible for cozy low-poly gameplay. ## Screenshot source policy

The eight listing screenshots use actual Unity gameplay/interface captures, with English editorial typography. No AI-generated substitute gameplay, invented controls or unsupported feature claims were used. Existing in-game watercolor art is used only as collection or story art.

Final typography, layout, exact export dimensions and color modes are produced by the editable Remotion project. PNG color-mode conversion is deterministic export processing.
