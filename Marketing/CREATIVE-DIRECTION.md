# Riverlight — English launch campaign

## Direction

A quiet river. A powerful reason to cast again. Teal water, warm brass, paper-white type and the light of a cabin window. The campaign combines the game's actual playable scenes with a clearly separate illustrated identity. No prominent human faces; characters appear from behind or under their hat. The icon contains only a golden carp and river water.

## Campaign hook

**One old journal. Five living legends.**

The first seconds establish a mystery, then immediately show fishing. The payoff is an invitation to continue Grandpa's unfinished story.

## Verified product basis

- Riverlight, a portrait fishing adventure for Android.
- Four playable locations: Riverlight River, Willow Lake, Pinefall Gorge and Saltreed Estuary.
- 48 fish species; five story legends.
- Cast, wait for a bite and manage tension while reeling.
- Journal, narrated memories, cabin decorations and equipment progression.
- Day and night change fishing opportunities.

Sources: Docs/GameDesign.md; Assets/Scripts/Fishing; Assets/Scripts/Core/GameState.cs; actual Unity captures. No invented multiplayer, offline, ad-free or award claims.
