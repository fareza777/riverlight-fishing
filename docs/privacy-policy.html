Riverlight: Fishing RPG

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

CozyFishing (“we”, “us”, or “our”) made Riverlight: Fishing RPG as a free-to-play mobile game. This policy explains what information is used when you play the game and the choices available to you.

Information stored on your device

Your fishing progress, settings, language choice, and the Remove Ads entitlement are stored locally on your device. We do not require an account and we do not operate a server that stores your save file.

Advertising

Riverlight may show interstitial or rewarded advertising through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may process device identifiers, app activity, diagnostics, and approximate location or other information permitted by your device and consent choices to provide, measure, and protect advertising. Where required, Google’s consent tools are shown in the game before personalized advertising is requested. You can manage available choices from the game’s Ad privacy choices setting.

When you buy Remove Ads, advertising requests are disabled for the game on that device. Rewarded advertising is also disabled after the entitlement is restored.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details. Google Play provides us with purchase status and transaction information needed to grant or restore the item, such as the one-time Remove Ads entitlement.

Information we do not request

Riverlight does not ask for your contacts, microphone, camera, precise location, or photos. We do not sell personal information.

Children

Riverlight is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact the developer through the support contact shown on the Google Play listing.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The latest version will remain available at this URL. For privacy questions or requests, use the developer support contact shown on the Riverlight Google Play listing.
